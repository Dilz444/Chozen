# Product photos on your phone

Same spot, same light, same way, every time. That's what makes a shop look like one shop.
A printable one-page version is in `photo-guide.pdf`.

## 1. Light

1. Shoot by a big window in daylight. Around midday or on a cloudy day is best.
2. Keep direct sun off the product. If the sun's coming in, hang a white sheet or net curtain over the window.
3. Flash off. Room lights off too, they turn photos yellow.

## 2. Backdrop

1. One plain warm-white or ivory board or sheet, behind and under the product. Use the same one every time.
2. Prop a second white board opposite the window. It bounces light back into the shadows.

## 3. Set up once, then repeat

1. Pick one spot and mark it with tape: where the product sits and where the phone goes.
2. Same distance, same height, every time. Put the phone on a stack of books or a cheap tripod.
3. Camera settings: gridlines on, 1x lens (not 0.5x), no filters.
4. Tap the product to focus, then slide the little sun down a touch so whites don't glare.
5. Product in the middle, filling about 70% of the frame.

## 4. Four shots per product

1. **The main photo:** square, straight on, with the phone at the product's middle height. Every product needs one.
2. **From above:** about 45°, looking down.
3. **A detail:** the stems, the stone, the clasp, the ribbon.
4. **Size:** in your hand or on a table, so people can see how big it really is.

## 5. Name the files

1. Use the product name, lowercase, hyphens, no spaces: `blush-rose-bouquet-1.jpg`.
2. `-1` is always the square main photo. Then `-2`, `-3`, `-4`.
3. Write the same names in the "Photo filenames" column of the spreadsheet.

## 6. Crystals and jewellery

1. Use a dark slate, or the same ivory board.
2. Show the size with a coin or your hand.
3. Wipe off dust and fingerprints first.

## 7. Balloons

1. Shoot outdoors in the shade, or against a plain wall.
2. Get the whole bundle in, weight and all.

## 8. Delivery photos

1. Always ask before photographing the person receiving the flowers.
2. If in doubt, photograph the bouquet on the doorstep only: no faces, no house numbers.

## Please don't

- Use filters or beauty modes.
- Put text, logos or stickers on photos.
- Shoot on busy backgrounds (kitchen worktops, patterned bedding).
- Use stock photos or pictures from the internet.

**Sending them:** full size, straight from the phone, by email or a shared folder. WhatsApp shrinks photos.
