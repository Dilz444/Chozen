ChoZen Boutique · photo guide

Product photos on your phone

Same spot, same light, same way, every time. That's what makes a shop look like one shop.

01Light

  1. Shoot by a big window in daylight. Around midday or a cloudy day is best.
  2. No direct sun on the product. If it's coming in, hang a white sheet or net curtain over the window.
  3. Flash off. Room lights off too, they turn photos yellow.

02Backdrop

  1. One plain warm-white or ivory board or sheet, behind and under the product. The same one every time.
  2. Prop a second white board opposite the window. It bounces light back into the shadows.

03Set up once, then repeat

  1. Pick one spot and mark it with tape: where the product sits and where the phone goes.
  2. Same distance, same height. Phone on a stack of books or a cheap tripod.
  3. Gridlines on, 1x lens (not 0.5x), no filters.
  4. Tap the product to focus, then slide the little sun down a touch so whites don't glare.
  5. Product in the middle, filling about 70% of the frame.

04Four shots per product

1. Main photoSquare, straight on, phone at the product's middle height. Every product has one.
2. From aboveAbout 45°, looking down on it.
3. A detailClose in on the stems, the stone, the clasp.
4. SizeIn your hand or on a table, so people see how big it is.

05Name the files

  1. Product name, lowercase, hyphens: blush-rose-bouquet-1.jpg
  2. -1 is always the square main photo, then -2, -3, -4.
  3. Write the same names in the spreadsheet's Photo filenames column.

06Crystals and jewellery

  1. A dark slate, or the same ivory board.
  2. Show the size with a coin or your hand.
  3. Wipe off dust and fingerprints first.

07Balloons

  1. Outdoors in the shade, or against a plain wall.
  2. Get the whole bundle in, weight and all.

08Delivery photos

  1. Always ask before photographing the person receiving the flowers.
  2. If in doubt, just the bouquet on the doorstep: no faces, no house numbers.

Please don't

  • Use filters or beauty modes
  • Put text, logos or stickers on photos
  • Shoot on busy backgrounds (worktops, patterned bedding)
  • Use stock photos or pictures from the internet
Sending them: full size, straight from the phone, by email or a shared folder. WhatsApp shrinks photos. One square main photo per product
