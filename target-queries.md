# Target queries

Every query family maps to **exactly one** page (the Mayfair method, playbook S2-01): no two pages compete for the
same search, and every page has a job. Evidence and sources are in `research/keywords.md` (clusters, SERPs,
confidence) and `research/competitors.md` (who ranks, what they charge). No search volumes exist here: no keyword
tool was available, and the search tool is US-biased. Demand is judged from who ranks.

**Re-check after launch** with Search Console (Performance → queries × pages) and retune each page toward the
queries it actually gets impressions for (playbook S4-09, S4-10).

## Read this first

- **"Enfield" means Connecticut to a search engine** unless told otherwise. Titles, H1s and schema say "Enfield,
  London" or carry a postcode, and the Google Business Profile does the heavy lifting.
- **The brand is crowded.** "ChoZen Boutique" returns a Cincinnati clothing shop. Brand queries need "Enfield" or
  "florist" until her profile and reviews build up.
- **The local results are thin, not strong.** Page 1 for Enfield and its neighbourhoods is mostly a network of about
  24 near-identical keyword-domain sites (floristenfield.co.uk, flowersenfieldtown.co.uk, flowerswinchmorehill.co.uk
  and so on) plus relay templates. Real shops with a profile, photos and reviews beat them in the map pack.
- **Her cut-off is the weak spot** (10am against 1pm–6pm). Pages lead on hand delivery and balloons, not speed.

**Where ChoZen wins:**
- **Flowers and balloons together, same day, hand-delivered**: none of the five does it. Sekret Garden on Baker
  Street sells both but has no website we could find.
- **A named florist with a story**, and photos of what she actually makes, against Interflora catalogue images and
  programmatic area pages.
- **Delivery fees published by postcode upfront**: several competitors hide them until checkout or contradict
  themselves.
- **Gift-led crystals**: a crystal chosen to go with a bouquet, which commodity crystal sellers don't offer.
- **Monthly floral hire** for local businesses: no local competitor found advertising it (unverified).

**Where she can't compete (and shouldn't try):** cut-off time (unless she moves it), Sunday same-day, price floors
on bouquets, tumblestones and bracelets, review counts in year one, and national head terms.
