# Focus: what to lead with, what to demote

**Short answer: your hypothesis is right, with three corrections.**

1. Fresh flowers and balloons, delivered the same day in North London, are the front door. ✔
2. **Lead on "hand-delivered, with balloons", not on speed.** Her 10am cut-off is the earliest we found. The nearest
   real Enfield florist (Flower, Lancaster Road) takes same-day orders to 1pm, the keyword-domain networks and
   Interflora to 3pm, and Flower Station to 5–6pm (research/competitors.md). A page that leads on "same day" loses
   that comparison. A page that leads on "flowers and balloons made up in Enfield and brought to the door by the
   florist" has no direct local competitor: we found **no** Enfield seller offering same-day fresh flowers and
   balloons together online (research/keywords.md, research/competitors.md). **Ask her whether noon or 1pm is
   workable** (B6). It's the single cheapest conversion gain available.
3. **Faux flowers are the nationwide second line, ahead of crystals.** Faux arrangements suit Google free Shopping
   listings, Pinterest and "gifts that last", carry a decent price, ship flat, and pair with the florist story.
   Crystals face a price floor she can't meet (43p tumblestones at Holistic Shop, £2.99 Etsy bracelets from a seller
   with 22,000 reviews), mature content competitors, and the strictest advertising rules of anything she sells. Keep
   crystals, but as **gift-meaning sets and single pieces she's chosen**, not an encyclopaedia she has to out-write.

## What the homepage does, in order

1. Says what she does, where, and by when: flowers and balloons, hand-delivered in Enfield today.
2. Lets you check your postcode before you scroll.
3. Shows today's flowers (real products, square photos, prices from £).
4. Sells the flowers-and-balloons pairing.
5. Offers "gifts that last, sent UK-wide" for everyone outside the area.
6. Introduces Beyzan.
7. Explains same-day in three steps, lists the areas, answers the four questions people ask.

The mockup (`mockup/index.html`) follows this order.

## Where I'd push back on the brief

- **Seven lines on the homepage is too many.** The mockup shows two lines prominently (fresh, balloons), three as one
  row (faux, crystals, jewellery), and none of subscription, hire or buy-back above the footer.
- **The crystal content is the biggest legal exposure and the least winnable SEO.** Don't port it 1:1. Port the facts
  and the gift meanings, drop the effects (`compliance/crystals.md`).
- **The subscription can wait** until the January 2027 rules are known, and until she knows how many deliveries a day she can make.
