## Google Business Profile (do this well; it's most of the local result)

- **Name:** "ChoZen Boutique" exactly. No keywords ("ChoZen Boutique Florist Enfield" breaks Google's rules and gets profiles suspended).
- **Primary category: Florist.** Secondary only if true: Balloon store, Gift shop. (Crystal shop or Jewellery store only if those are a real part of what walk-in customers buy.)
- **Address:** if customers can visit, show it. If she works from home, set it as a **service-area business** with the address hidden, and service areas Enfield, Winchmore Hill, Palmers Green, Southgate, Edmonton, Enfield Lock (her confirmed list, up to 20).
- **Hours:** her real order hours. Mark Sundays and bank holidays honestly.
- **Products:** her top 10 bouquets and balloon sets with photos and prices (or synced from Merchant Center).
- **Photos:** 10 at the start (her, her workspace, 6–8 real bouquets and balloon sets), then **2–3 a week**, every week, straight from her phone. Fresh photos are one of the few profile signals she controls.
- **Posts:** one a week (a new seasonal bouquet, a delivery area reminder, Mother's Day ordering date).
- **Reviews:** ask every customer (see below). Reply to every review within two days, in her voice, without arguing.
- **Questions:** the old Q&A feature is gone; Google now writes answers from the profile, reviews and the website, so the website's delivery page and FAQs matter here too.
- **Don't:** use a virtual office or PO box as the address, add keywords to the name, buy reviews, or review herself.

## Reviews after every order

1. Judge.me emails every customer automatically (product review on the site).
2. The same email has a second button: "Happy with your flowers? A Google review helps a small shop more than anything." Link from GBP → "Ask for reviews".
3. A small card in every local delivery with a QR code to the Google review link (Dilz makes a print file).
4. **Never** offer a discount for a review, never filter who gets asked, never write one for a friend. Publish the review policy (DMCC Act).
5. Target: 10 Google reviews in the first 60 days, 25 by day 90.

## Email

- **Abandoned checkout:** on from day one (free). One email after an hour: what they left, the cut-off, one line from her. No discount by default.
- **Newsletter:** monthly, plus the seasonal dates. Sign-up on the site with a plain promise ("new seasonal flowers and the dates to remember, a few times a month"). Customers can be emailed under the soft opt-in (unticked opt-out at checkout, unsubscribe in every email). **Gift recipients are never emailed.**
- **Date reminders:** "Remind me next year" checkbox on birthday and anniversary orders (consented), one email ten days before.
