## Who is responsible for my data?

[LEGAL NAME], trading as [TRADING NAME], is the controller of your personal data. Write to us at [ADDRESS] or email hello@chozenboutique.co.uk [to confirm]. We're registered with the Information Commissioner's Office, registration number [ICO NUMBER].

## What personal details do you collect about me?

- **Your order:** name, email, phone number, billing and delivery addresses, what you bought and when.
- **Payment:** handled by our payment provider. We see the last four digits of your card and whether payment went through, never the full card number.
- **Your account,** if you create one: login email and order history.
- **Messages:** anything you send us by email, contact form or chat, including hire enquiries.
- **Newsletter:** your email address and when you signed up.
- **Reviews:** your name and what you write, if you leave a review.
- **Website use:** pages viewed and device type, through cookies (see our [cookie policy](/pages/cookies)).

## What do you collect about the person receiving my gift?

When you send a gift, you give us the recipient's name, delivery address, any phone number or delivery notes you add, and your card message. We use these only to make up and deliver the order and to contact the recipient about that delivery. We never add recipients to our mailing list or use their details for marketing. Their details are kept as part of your order record. Please only give us what we need to deliver.

## What legal basis do you have for using my data?

| What we do | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Take payment, make up and deliver your order, handle returns | Contract |
| Deliver to and contact a gift recipient | Legitimate interests (delivering the gift you've paid for) |
| Keep order and tax records | Legal obligation |
| Answer your questions and hire enquiries | Contract, or legitimate interests if you haven't ordered |
| Email customers about similar products, with an unsubscribe link in every email | Legitimate interests (you can opt out at checkout or any time) |
| Send our newsletter to people who signed up without ordering | Consent |
| Ask for a review after your order | Legitimate interests |
| Count visits with first-party statistics | Legitimate interests (you can opt out) |
| Marketing cookies and advertising pixels | Consent, through the cookie banner |
| Prevent fraud | Legitimate interests |

## Who do you share my data with?

Only the companies that help us run the shop, under contracts that limit what they can do with it:

- **Shopify**, which hosts our shop, checkout and customer records, and sends our emails through Shopify Email
- **Payment providers:** Shopify Payments (run with Stripe) and PayPal [to confirm]
- **[Delivery app, to confirm]**, which handles delivery dates and postcodes
- **Royal Mail** [to confirm], for parcels posted around the UK
- **Judge.me**, which sends review requests and shows reviews
- **Google Analytics**, only if you accept analytics cookies [to confirm whether used]

We also share data where the law requires it, for example with HMRC.

## Does my data leave the UK?

Yes. Shopify and some of the companies above store data in Canada and the United States. The UK recognises Canada as giving adequate protection. For the US, the companies rely on the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK's International Data Transfer Addendum [to confirm per provider].

## What are my rights?

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your data
- correct it
- delete it, where we don't need to keep it by law
- limit how we use it, or object to it, including stopping all marketing at any time
- send it to you or another company in a common format
- withdraw consent you've given, without affecting what happened before

Email us and we'll reply within one month. It's free.

## How do I complain?

Please tell us first and we'll try to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.
