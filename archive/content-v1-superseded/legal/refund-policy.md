## What can I send back if I change my mind?

Faux flowers, crystals, jewellery and gifts can be cancelled for any reason within 14 days of the day they arrive, with you or with the person you sent them to. You don't need to tell us why. Once you've told us, you have a further 14 days to post the items back.

## Why can't fresh flowers be returned?

Fresh flowers start to fade from the day they're cut, so they can't be sent back and sold again. The law treats them as perishable and leaves them out of the change-of-mind right, and that includes balloon gift sets with fresh flowers. If your flowers arrive damaged, wilting or not as described, you're still fully covered (see below).

## Which other items can't be returned?

- **Personalised items.** Anything engraved, printed with a name or made to your own design. Items from our range that we make up when you order can still be returned.
- **Earrings for pierced ears** that we send sealed, once the seal is broken, for hygiene reasons [to confirm].

## Can I return balloons?

Balloons on their own can be cancelled within 14 days like any keepsake [to confirm]. If a balloon arrives burst or flat, tell us the same day and we'll replace it.

## How do I cancel?

Email us at hello@chozenboutique.co.uk [to confirm] with your order number and the items you're cancelling. You can also fill in the form at the end and email or post it to us. Then post the items, unused and in their packaging if you still have it, to [ADDRESS].

## Who pays to send items back?

You pay the postage to return items you've changed your mind about, unless they're faulty or we sent the wrong thing [to confirm].

## When will I get my money back?

We refund you within 14 days of receiving the items back, or of you showing us proof you've posted them, whichever comes first. The refund includes the delivery charge you paid and goes back to the card or account you paid with. If an item comes back used beyond what you'd need to look at it, we may take off an amount for the loss in value.

## What if something is faulty or not as described?

You have rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 whatever the item and whatever the reason you bought it. Goods must be of satisfactory quality, fit for purpose and as described.

- For a keepsake, you can reject it for a full refund within 30 days of it arriving.
- For fresh flowers, that right lasts as long as the flowers could reasonably be expected to last.
- After that, you're entitled to a repair or a replacement, and if that doesn't fix it, a price reduction or a refund.

We pay the return postage on faulty items, and refund you within 14 days of agreeing a refund.

## What should I do if flowers arrive damaged?

Get in touch the same day if you can, by email or on [PHONE], and tell us what's wrong. A photo of the bouquet helps us see what happened and fix it quickly, so please send one if you're able. It's a request, not a condition, and you're covered either way. We'll offer a replacement or a refund.

## What about my flower subscription?

You can cancel future deliveries at any time online [to confirm how]. A delivery that's already arrived is covered by the fresh flower rule above, and by your rights if it's faulty.

## What about floral hire?

Hire bookings have their own cancellation terms, set out in our [terms of service](/policies/terms-of-service#floral-hire).

## Can a gift recipient return something?

Yes, they can contact us directly. Any refund goes back to the person who paid for the order.
