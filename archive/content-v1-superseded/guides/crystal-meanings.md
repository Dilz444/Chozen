## What do the most popular crystals mean?

### Amethyst

Purple quartz, coloured by traces of iron, and February's birthstone. It's traditionally associated with calm
and clear thinking, and its Greek name means "not drunk". [Amethyst meaning](/blogs/guides/amethyst-meaning)

### Rose quartz

Milky pink quartz that rarely forms visible crystals. In crystal lore it's the stone of love, so it's the classic
gift for a partner, a mum or a best friend. [Rose quartz meaning](/blogs/guides/rose-quartz-meaning)

### Clear quartz

Colourless quartz in six-sided points, also called rock crystal and the alternative April birthstone in the UK.
Crystal lore calls it the "master crystal", a stone of clarity. [Clear quartz meaning](/blogs/guides/clear-quartz-meaning)

### Citrine

Yellow to orange quartz, known in folklore as the merchant's stone. Most citrine on sale is heat-treated
amethyst, so the label matters. [Citrine meaning](/blogs/guides/citrine-meaning)

### Black tourmaline

A glossy black crystal ridged along its length, found in Cornish granite as well as Brazil and Namibia. It's
traditionally regarded as a protective, grounding stone for the front door. [Black tourmaline meaning](/blogs/guides/black-tourmaline-meaning)

### Tiger's eye

Gold and brown striped quartz with a band of light that slides as you turn it. It's linked with courage, and it's a
popular good-luck token before exams and interviews. [Tiger's eye meaning](/blogs/guides/tigers-eye-meaning)

### Labradorite

A grey feldspar that flashes blue and gold at certain angles. Crystal lore links it with change and new chapters,
which suits a leaving gift. [Labradorite meaning](/blogs/guides/labradorite-meaning)

### Moonstone

A feldspar with a pale glow that drifts under the surface, and the alternative June birthstone in the UK.
Traditionally it's tied to the moon, new beginnings and travellers. [Moonstone meaning](/blogs/guides/moonstone-meaning)

### Carnelian

Translucent orange chalcedony, used for Roman seal rings because wax doesn't stick to it. In crystal lore it's a
stone of courage and creativity, and it's July's alternative birthstone. [Carnelian meaning](/blogs/guides/carnelian-meaning)

### Green aventurine

Green quartz that glitters with flakes of chromium mica, mostly from India. Folklore knows it as a lucky stone for
fresh starts. [Green aventurine meaning](/blogs/guides/green-aventurine-meaning)

### Selenite

A soft, satiny white gypsum named after the Greek moon goddess. It's said to cleanse other crystals, and it must
never get wet. [Selenite meaning](/blogs/guides/selenite-meaning)

### Lapis lazuli

Deep blue rock flecked with gold-coloured pyrite, mined in Afghanistan for six thousand years. It's linked with
wisdom and is September's alternative birthstone. [Lapis lazuli meaning](/blogs/guides/lapis-lazuli-meaning)

### Smoky quartz

Quartz in shades from pale brown to near black, darkened by natural radiation in the rock. The Scottish variety,
Cairngorm, was set in Highland brooches, and in crystal lore it's a grounding stone. Much smoky quartz on sale is
clear quartz irradiated to darken it, which a careful label states. [Crystals collection](/collections/crystals)

### Fluorite

Banded purple, green and blue crystal, soft at 4 on the Mohs scale. Derbyshire's Blue John is a rare banded
fluorite. Crystal lore associates fluorite with clear thinking, so it's a favourite desk stone for students.
[Crystals collection](/collections/crystals)

### Obsidian

Volcanic glass that cooled too fast to form crystals. Polished obsidian mirrors were made in Aztec Mexico, and one
that belonged to the Elizabethan scholar John Dee is in the British Museum. In crystal lore it's a protective,
reflective stone. [Crystals collection](/collections/crystals)

## Which crystals come in which colour?

- **Pink:** rose quartz, rhodonite, pink opal
- **Purple:** amethyst, fluorite, lepidolite
- **Blue:** lapis lazuli, sodalite, blue lace agate, aquamarine
- **Green:** green aventurine, jade, malachite, fluorite
- **Yellow and orange:** citrine, carnelian, tiger's eye
- **Black and brown:** black tourmaline, obsidian, smoky quartz
- **White and clear:** clear quartz, selenite, moonstone, howlite
- **Changing colour:** labradorite, moonstone

The [crystals by colour guide](/blogs/guides/crystals-by-colour) explains what makes each colour and which stones
fade in sunlight.

## What's the difference between tumbled, raw, cluster, point, sphere and tower?

The shape is about how the stone was finished, not what it is. The same amethyst can be sold in all six.

- **Tumbled:** small stones turned in a barrel with grit for several weeks until smooth and glossy, usually two to
  four centimetres across. Pocket-sized, easy to post, and the most affordable way to give a crystal.
- **Raw (or rough):** pieces as they came out of the ground, broken but not polished. You see the stone's natural
  texture, and no two are alike.
- **Cluster:** many crystals growing from one shared base, exactly as they formed. Amethyst, clear quartz and
  citrine are the common ones. A shelf piece rather than a pocket one.
- **Point:** a single crystal with a pointed tip. Some are natural crystals. Many are cut and polished into a
  six-sided point, which is a carving rather than a natural shape.
- **Sphere:** a stone carved and polished into a ball, usually sold with a small stand. A clear quartz sphere can
  focus sunlight and scorch fabric, so keep spheres out of direct sun.
- **Tower:** a polished column with a pointed top that stands upright on a shelf or desk. It's a carved shape, so
  it shows off the colour and banding of the stone more than its crystal form.

Hearts and palm stones are carved too. A palm stone is a smooth, flattened oval that sits in the hand.

## How do you choose a crystal as a gift?

Start with the person rather than the meaning. Their birth month gives you a birthstone (see the
[birthstones by month guide](/blogs/guides/birthstones-by-month)), their star sign gives you a fun pairing (see
[zodiac crystals](/blogs/guides/zodiac-crystals)), and their favourite colour narrows the rest. Then pick a shape
that suits where it'll live, a tumble stone for a card or a pocket, a cluster or tower for a shelf. The [crystals collection](/collections/crystals) filters by stone, colour and shape. Write the
meaning on the gift card. It's the part people remember.
