## What is the traditional flower for each anniversary?

This is the list most often used in the UK. The flower meanings are traditional associations, not rules.

| Anniversary | Flower | Traditionally a symbol of |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | Carnation | Young love and devotion |
| 2nd | Lily of the valley | Sweetness and happiness |
| 3rd | Sunflower | Warmth and loyalty |
| 4th | Hydrangea | Gratitude and understanding |
| 5th | Daisy | Faithfulness and simple love |
| 6th | Calla lily | Admiration |
| 7th | Freesia | Trust |
| 8th | Lilac | Joy in the relationship |
| 9th | Bird of paradise | Adventure |
| 10th | Daffodil | Happiness and new beginnings |
| 11th | Tulip | Devoted love |
| 12th | Peony | A happy marriage |
| 13th | Chrysanthemum | Friendship and loyalty |
| 14th | Dendrobium orchid | Love and beauty |
| 15th | Rose | Love |
| 20th | Aster | Patience |
| 25th | Iris | Hope and wisdom |
| 28th | Orchid | Lasting love |
| 30th | Lily | Devotion |
| 40th | Gladiolus | Strength of character |
| 50th | Yellow rose and violet | Shared years and faithfulness |

## Where do the lists disagree?

Mostly from the fourth year on. The common differences are:

- **4th:** hydrangea in most UK lists, geranium or gerbera in some.
- **14th:** dendrobium orchid, or dahlia.
- **Years 16 to 24:** many lists skip these, so there's no fixed flower.
- **60th:** some lists don't give a flower at all, and people often choose white flowers to go with the diamond theme.

The older tradition only marked a handful of years, mainly the 1st, 5th, 10th, 15th, 20th, 25th and 50th. The
in-between years were added later by gift sellers. So if two lists disagree, neither is wrong.

## How does the flower list fit with paper, cotton and the other year gifts?

They're separate traditions, and you can pair them. The UK material list runs paper for the first, cotton for the
second, leather for the third, then wood for the fifth, tin for the tenth, crystal for the fifteenth, china for
the twentieth, silver for the twenty-fifth, pearl for the thirtieth, ruby for the fortieth and gold for the
fiftieth.

Some pairings that work well:

- **1st, paper and carnations.** A bunch of carnations with a handwritten letter.
- **10th, tin and daffodils.** Daffodils in a vintage tin.
- **15th, crystal and roses.** Roses alongside a crystal gift. Our [crystals](/collections/crystals) are posted UK-wide.
- **25th, silver and irises.** Irises with a silver-wrapped card.
- **40th, ruby and roses.** Deep red roses, the obvious match.
- **50th, gold and yellow roses.** Yellow roses for the gold anniversary.

## What if the flower is out of season?

Some of these flowers are only around for a few weeks. Lily of the valley is a spring flower, mostly April and May.
Peonies are at their best in May and June, and daffodils from late winter into spring. Lilac has a short season in
late spring.

When the traditional flower isn't available, choose its colour instead, or go for a faux version that keeps. Our
[faux flowers](/collections/faux-flowers) are posted UK-wide, so they suit couples who live further away.
