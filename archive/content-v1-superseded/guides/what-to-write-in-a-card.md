## How do I write a card message that sounds like me?

Start with their name, say the one thing you'd say if they were in front of you, and stop. A shared joke, a
place or a nickname makes it yours. If you're stuck, borrow one of the lines here and change a word or two.

## What should I write in a birthday card?

- Happy birthday, Sam. Here's to the year ahead being kind to you.
- Another lap of the sun. Thanks for making mine better.
- Happy birthday to the person who always remembers mine.
- Wishing you a slow morning, a long lunch and cake for dinner.
- Thirty looks good on you. Happy birthday.
- Sorry I can't be there to sing badly. Consider these the chorus.
- For the best part of my week, every week. Happy birthday.

## What should I write in an anniversary card?

- Five years, two flats and one very spoilt dog. Happy anniversary.
- I'd still pick you. Every time.
- Happy anniversary to my favourite person to do nothing with.
- Thank you for all the ordinary days. They're my favourites.
- Here's to us, and to many more years of you stealing the duvet.
- Still laughing at your jokes, even the old ones. Love you.

## What should I write for a new baby?

- Hello, little one. You picked a lovely family to land in.
- Congratulations on your beautiful boy. We can't wait to meet him.
- She's here. So much love to all three of you.
- Congratulations. Rest when you can, and call us when you need us.
- Ten tiny fingers, ten tiny toes and a whole lot of love waiting for them.
- Welcome, baby Ava. Your aunties are ready to spoil you.

## What should I write in a thank you card?

- Thank you for everything this week. I couldn't have done it without you.
- A small thank you for a very big favour.
- You went out of your way, and I noticed. Thank you.
- Thanks for the lift, the tea and the pep talk.
- To the best neighbours on the road. Thank you for keeping an eye on things.
- Thank you for making the day so special. We'll talk about it for years.

## What should I write in a get well card?

- Thinking of you and hoping each day is a little easier.
- Rest up and let everyone else do the running around for once.
- Sending love and something to look at that isn't the ceiling.
- Get well soon. The office is far too quiet without you.
- Take all the time you need. We're here when you're ready.
- Here's to feeling more like yourself very soon.

## What should I write in a sympathy card?

Keep it short and simple. You don't need to find the right words, because there aren't any. Saying you're
thinking of them is enough.

- Thinking of you all, with love.
- With deepest sympathy, and fond memories of John.
- We're so sorry. Sending you all our love.
- Remembering Margaret with love. She'll be so missed.
- Holding you close in our thoughts.
- With love and sympathy at this sad time.
- No need to reply. We're thinking of you.

## What should I write in a Mother's Day card?

- Happy Mother's Day, Mum. Thank you for everything, especially the bits I never noticed.
- To the woman who taught me to make a proper cup of tea. Love you.
- I wish I could be there today. Sending you a hug in flower form.
- Happy Mother's Day to my second mum. You've always made me feel at home.
- Thank you for the lifts, the packed lunches and the patience.
- Happy first Mother's Day. You're doing brilliantly.

## What should I write for Valentine's Day?

- You, me and a takeaway. That's all I want tonight.
- Still my favourite hello.

- Every day with you is the good bit.
- For the one who makes ordinary days feel like weekends.

## What should I write when it's just because?

- No reason. Just thinking of you.
- Saw these and thought of you.
- A little something to brighten your Tuesday.
- Because you deserve flowers on a normal day too.
- Sending a smile your way.
- You've had a hard few weeks. These are to say I see you.
