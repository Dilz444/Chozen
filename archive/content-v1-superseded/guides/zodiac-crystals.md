Star-sign dates are the usual ones and can move by a day from year to year. Each sign has a stone that
comes up often in modern crystal lists, and a second if you'd like a choice.

## Which crystal goes with Aries?

Carnelian is the stone most often paired with Aries (21 March to 19 April), a fire sign. Its warm orange and its
crystal-lore link with courage suit the sign's reputation for charging in first. Red jasper is the usual second
choice. See the [carnelian guide](/blogs/guides/carnelian-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Taurus?

Rose quartz, for Taurus (20 April to 20 May), an earth sign ruled in astrology by Venus. Green stones such as
green aventurine and emerald are the other common picks. A rose quartz heart is an easy May birthday gift. See the
[rose quartz guide](/blogs/guides/rose-quartz-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Gemini?

Citrine is the usual stone for Gemini (21 May to 20 June), an air sign. Its sunny yellow fits the sign's chatty,
curious reputation in astrology. Agate, in its many bands and colours, is the other classic. See the
[citrine guide](/blogs/guides/citrine-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Cancer?

Moonstone, for Cancer (21 June to 22 July), the water sign ruled by the Moon in astrology. Pearl, June's
birthstone, is the other common pairing, so a moonstone suits a late-June birthday twice over. See the
[moonstone guide](/blogs/guides/moonstone-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Leo?

Tiger's eye is the stone most often paired with Leo (23 July to 22 August), a fire sign ruled by the Sun. The
gold banding is the obvious match for the lion. Citrine and peridot are the alternatives. See the
[tiger's eye guide](/blogs/guides/tigers-eye-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Virgo?

Green aventurine and peridot are the common pairings for Virgo (23 August to 22 September), an earth sign. Some
lists give sapphire, September's birthstone. A small green stone for a desk suits the sign's tidy, practical
reputation. See the [green aventurine guide](/blogs/guides/green-aventurine-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Libra?

Lapis lazuli, for Libra (23 September to 22 October), an air sign ruled by Venus. Rose quartz and opal also
appear on many lists. Lapis's deep blue and gold are a good match for the sign of the scales. See the
[lapis lazuli guide](/blogs/guides/lapis-lazuli-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Scorpio?

Obsidian and labradorite are the stones most often paired with Scorpio (23 October to 21 November), a water sign
with a reputation for intensity. Topaz, November's birthstone, is the traditional alternative. See the
[labradorite guide](/blogs/guides/labradorite-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Sagittarius?

Turquoise, for Sagittarius (22 November to 21 December), the fire sign of the traveller in astrology.
Turquoise has been worn by travellers across Persia and the American Southwest, and it's also December's
birthstone. Lapis lazuli and sodalite are the other blue picks.

## Which crystal goes with Capricorn?

Garnet and smoky quartz are the usual stones for Capricorn (22 December to 19 January), an earth sign. Black
tourmaline appears on many lists too. Garnet doubles as January's birthstone. See the
[black tourmaline guide](/blogs/guides/black-tourmaline-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Aquarius?

Amethyst is the stone most often paired with Aquarius (20 January to 18 February), an air sign. It's also
February's birthstone, so it works for most Aquarius birthdays. Aquamarine, whose name shares the sign's
water-bearer theme, is the alternative. See the [amethyst guide](/blogs/guides/amethyst-meaning).

## Which crystal goes with Pisces?

Aquamarine and amethyst, for Pisces (19 February to 20 March), the last water sign. Aquamarine is March's
birthstone and amethyst February's, so between them they cover the sign's dates. Moonstone is a softer third
choice.

## How do you give a zodiac crystal?

Keep it light. A tumble stone with the sign written on the card is a small, fun extra with flowers or a birthday
present. If you're unsure of their sign, a birthstone or a favourite colour works just as well. See the
[crystals collection](/collections/crystals) and the [birthstones guide](/blogs/guides/birthstones-by-month).
