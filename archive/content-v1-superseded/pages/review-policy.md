## How are reviews collected?

After every order, Judge.me sends you an email asking how it went [to confirm: how many days after delivery]. Everyone gets the same email, whatever they ordered and however we think it went. Only people who've placed an order can leave a review on our site, and each one is marked as a verified buyer [to confirm].

## Are any reviews paid for or rewarded?

No. We don't offer discounts, free gifts or prize draws for reviews. If that ever changes, every review given in return for a reward will be clearly labelled, and we'll say so here.

## Can staff, friends or family leave reviews?

No. Nobody who works for the shop, and none of their friends or family, reviews our products.

## Are bad reviews published?

Yes. We publish every review, including low ratings and complaints. We may reply in public, but we never edit what a reviewer wrote. We only remove a review if it's unlawful, abusive or discriminatory, shares someone's personal details, has nothing to do with our products or service, or is spam. Removal is decided case by case and the rating isn't a factor.

## How do I report a review I think is fake?

Email hello@chozenboutique.co.uk [to confirm] with a link to the review and why you think it isn't genuine. We'll look into it within [to confirm] and take it down if it isn't. You can also report a business you think is using fake reviews to the Citizens Advice consumer helpline on 0808 223 1133.

## What about reviews on Google?

Reviews on Google are run by Google. We can reply to them, and we'll flag any we think break Google's rules, but only Google can remove them.
