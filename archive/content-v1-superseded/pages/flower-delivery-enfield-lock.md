## Which neighbourhoods does EN3 delivery include?

Five, all along the eastern side of Enfield [to confirm]:

- **Ponders End**, around the Hertford Road and the station.
- **Brimsdown**, including the industrial estates by the river.
- **Enfield Highway**, either side of the Hertford Road.
- **Enfield Wash**, up towards Turkey Street.
- **Enfield Lock**, with Enfield Island Village on the old Royal Small Arms Factory site by the River Lee.

## Is EN3 covered on the same day as Enfield Town?

Yes. EN3 is on the same daily round [to confirm], so a morning order arrives that day like anywhere else we
cover. Delivery days and fees are on the [delivery page](/pages/delivery).

[her notes to come: what she's sent to EN3]. A balloon set ties to a banister or a chair back, so it needs no

## Can I order for a birthday in EN3 the day before?

Of course. Pick tomorrow's date at checkout and it goes out on that morning's round. See
[birthday flowers](/collections/birthday-flowers) for ideas, or add [balloons](/pages/balloon-delivery-enfield).
