## Why hand-deliver instead of posting?

Fresh flowers look their best on the day they're made up, so they go to the door by hand, not in a box through
the post. That's why fresh flowers and balloons stay in Enfield and the nearby parts of North London. Faux
arrangements, crystals and jewellery keep well in a parcel, so those go anywhere in the UK.

## Why crystals and jewellery alongside flowers?

Some gifts are meant to fade with the week and some are meant to stay on a shelf for years. I wanted both in one
place, so you can send roses for today and a piece of rose quartz to keep. [her words to come: how she came to
crystals]
