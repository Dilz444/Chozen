## What is a cookie?

A small file a website saves in your browser so it can remember things between pages, such as what's in your basket.

## Which cookies are always on?

The ones the shop can't work without. They keep your basket, carry you through checkout, keep you signed in, protect against fraud and remember your cookie choice. The law doesn't require consent for these, and turning them off in your browser will stop the basket working.

## Do you track my visit?

We count visits with first-party statistics: which pages are viewed, how people arrive and whether checkout works. The figures stay with us and Shopify and aren't used for advertising. You can switch statistics off at any time in **Cookie settings** at the bottom of every page [to confirm].

## When are marketing cookies used?

Only if you choose **Accept** in the cookie banner. These come from advertising services such as [to confirm: Meta, Google Ads, Pinterest] and let them show you our products elsewhere. If you choose **Reject**, none of them load.

## How do I change my mind?

Open **Cookie settings** at the bottom of any page and change your choice. You can also delete cookies in your browser settings.
