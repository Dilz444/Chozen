## Why choose a florist in Enfield rather than a national website?

Your bouquet is made up here and delivered by us [to confirm: Beyzan or her own driver], so it doesn't pass
through a courier on the way.
National sites pass the order to whichever florist has signed up nearby. With us, you know who chose the stems.

## Can you deliver flowers to Chase Farm Hospital?

Yes, to the main reception on The Ridgeway [to confirm]. Many wards won't take fresh flowers or latex balloons,
so ring the ward first. A small faux arrangement or a crystal in a gift box is often the gentler choice for a
hospital bedside, and both are in our [get well soon gifts](/collections/get-well-soon-gifts).

## Can you send sympathy flowers to a service in Enfield?

We can, with a little notice. Enfield Crematorium on Great Cambridge Road, Lavender Hill Cemetery on Cedar Road
and St Andrew's Church on the Market Place are all close by [to confirm]. Tell us the service time and the
funeral director, and we'll take the flowers to them. The [sympathy collection](/collections/sympathy-flowers)
has arrangements made in quiet whites and greens.
