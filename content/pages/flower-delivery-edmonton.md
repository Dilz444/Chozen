## Which parts of Edmonton do you deliver to?

All of N9 and N18 [to confirm]. Lower Edmonton (N9) takes in Edmonton Green, Church Street and the roads up
towards Bush Hill Park. Upper Edmonton (N18) runs down past Silver Street to Pymmes Park and Sterling Way.

## Can you deliver to North Middlesex University Hospital?

Yes, to the main reception on Sterling Way, N18 [to confirm]. We can't take flowers onto a ward, and plenty of
wards don't accept fresh flowers or latex balloons at all. Before you order, ring the ward or ask the person
you're sending to. Our [get well soon gifts](/collections/get-well-soon-gifts) include faux flowers and crystals
that suit a bedside where fresh stems aren't allowed.

## What do people send to Edmonton?

[her notes to come: what she sees ordered for N9 and N18]. For parents just home from the maternity unit at North
Middlesex, a [new baby gift](/collections/new-baby-gifts) with a foil balloon is easier to fit into a house full
of visitors than a big vase.

## Can you take flowers to a service at All Saints, Edmonton?

Yes, with notice. All Saints stands on Church Street, N9. Call 07931 662545 with the service time and the
funeral director or family contact, and we'll agree where to bring them [to confirm].
