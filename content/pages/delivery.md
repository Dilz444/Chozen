## Will the bouquet look like the photo?

It'll match the photo in style and colour. Flowers are seasonal, so if a stem isn't at its best on the day, we use another seasonal stem of equal value that keeps the same look.

## Can I send flowers and a keepsake in one order?

Yes, if both are going to the same address in our delivery area. The keepsake comes in the van with the flowers. Fresh flowers can't be posted, so if you'd like a keepsake sent to an address elsewhere in the UK, place it as its own order.

## Are lilies safe if the recipient has cats?

No. Every part of a lily is toxic to cats, including the pollen and the water in the vase. If the person you're sending to has a cat, say so in the order notes and we'll make the bouquet up without lilies.
