## Can you bring flowers to Southgate Green?

The Green itself has no letterbox, but the houses and churches around it do. Give us the full address and we'll
get it there. The conservation area around it also takes in Southgate Cemetery and the Walker Cricket Ground.
If the flowers need to arrive before a particular time, say so in the notes.
