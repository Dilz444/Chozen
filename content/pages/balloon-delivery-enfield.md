Yes. Choose a bouquet, add a balloon set, and one delivery brings both. It suits birthdays, new babies and exam

## How is a hand-delivered balloon different from a balloon in a box?

A posted balloon comes flat-packed or boxed, usually the next day. Ours are filled here in Enfield and carried
to the door on the day, so the first thing the person sees is the balloons, not a cardboard box.

## How do I look after the balloons once they arrive?

Keep them indoors, away from radiators, ceiling fans and strong sun. Foil balloons conduct electricity, so never
let one go outside near overhead power lines, and always keep the weight attached. Don't breathe in the helium.
