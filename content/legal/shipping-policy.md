## Anything else to know?

- **Lilies are toxic to cats.** Every part of the plant, including the pollen and the vase water. If the recipient has cats, tell us in the order notes and we'll leave lilies out.
- **Balloons.** Keep them away from overhead power lines, keep them weighted, never release them outdoors and don't inhale the helium. Latex balloons carry a choking warning for children under 8. Full safety notes are on the [delivery page](/pages/delivery).
