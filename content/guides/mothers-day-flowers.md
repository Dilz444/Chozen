## When is Mother's Day this year?

Mother's Day in 2027 is on Sunday 7 March.

It's always three weeks before Easter Sunday, which is why it moves around. In the UK it's also called Mothering
Sunday.

## When should I order Mother's Day flowers?

As early as you can, ideally a week or two before. The weekend before Mother's Day is one of the busiest of the
year for florists, and the most popular bouquets sell out. Ordering early also lets you choose the delivery day.

If you've left it late, local same-day delivery is the answer. See our [Mother's Day](/collections/mothers-day)
collection and [same-day flowers](/collections/same-day-flowers).

## What flowers are traditional for Mother's Day?

There's no single flower, but spring flowers are the natural choice because they're in season. Tulips, daffodils,
ranunculus, hyacinths and anemones are at their best in March. Pink and white carnations are an older Mother's Day
tradition. Roses in soft pinks and peaches are always a safe pick.

If your mum has a cat, ask for a bouquet without lilies. Our [flowers and pets guide](/blogs/guides/flowers-and-pets)
explains why.

## What can I send to a mum who lives far away?

A gift that travels well by post and keeps. Fresh flowers sent a long way can arrive tired, and a gift that lasts
is a reminder of you for weeks or years. Some ideas:

- **Faux flowers,** which keep their shape and colour for years with a dust now and then. See our [faux flowers](/collections/faux-flowers).
- **A crystal or a piece of gemstone jewellery,** posted UK-wide. Browse our [crystals](/collections/crystals) and other [gifts that last](/collections/gifts-that-last).
- **A handwritten letter in the parcel,** which costs nothing and is often what she keeps longest.

## What else goes well with flowers?

A balloon adds something cheerful for a mum who loves a fuss, and our
[balloon gift sets](/collections/balloon-gift-sets) pair flowers and balloons locally. Chocolates and a card are the
classic pairing. For grandmas, stepmums, mother figures or a friend who's a new mum, the same ideas work.

## What should I write in a Mother's Day card?

Thank her for something specific. A memory, a habit of hers or something she taught you means more than any
ready-made verse.

- Thank you for always picking up the phone.
- I still use your recipe every time. Happy Mother's Day.
- Happy Mother's Day to the person who taught me to be kind.
- Sorry I can't be there today. I'll see you soon, and I'm bringing cake.

There are more in our [card message ideas](/blogs/guides/what-to-write-in-a-card).

## How do I make Mother's Day flowers last?

Re-cut the stems, add flower food, change the water every two days and keep the vase out of the sun. Spring
flowers like tulips are fussier than most, so [tulip care](/blogs/guides/tulip-care) is worth a read. For
everything else, see [how to make flowers last](/blogs/guides/how-to-make-flowers-last).
