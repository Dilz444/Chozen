## What gives a crystal its colour?

Usually a small amount of another element. Pure quartz is colourless. Add traces of iron and natural radiation
and it becomes purple amethyst. Fibres of another mineral turn it pink. Some minerals carry their colour in their
own make-up instead, like copper in malachite and turquoise. Others get it from light playing on thin layers
inside the stone, which is why labradorite and opal flash.

## What are the pink crystals?

Rose quartz is the best-known, a milky pale pink (see the [rose quartz guide](/blogs/guides/rose-quartz-meaning)).
Rhodonite is a deeper pink with black veins of manganese oxide. Rhodochrosite is pink and white banded, soft, and
mostly from Argentina and Peru. Pink opal from Peru is chalky and pastel. In crystal lore pink stones are tied
to love and kindness, so they're the usual choice for Valentine's Day, Mother's Day and new babies.

## What are the purple crystals?

Amethyst is the classic purple, from pale lilac to deep violet (see the [amethyst guide](/blogs/guides/amethyst-meaning)).
Lepidolite is a lilac mica that glitters and flakes, coloured by lithium and manganese. Fluorite often comes in
purple bands with green. Purple stones are traditionally associated with calm and quiet, and they're
February's colour, as amethyst is the month's birthstone.

## What are the blue crystals?

Lapis lazuli is the deep blue with gold flecks (see the [lapis lazuli guide](/blogs/guides/lapis-lazuli-meaning)).
Sodalite looks similar but has white veins and no gold. Blue lace agate is pale blue with fine white bands,
aquamarine is a clear sea blue, and celestite forms soft, pale blue crystals in geodes. Turquoise sits between
blue and green. Blue stones are linked in crystal lore with honest speech and the sea.

## What are the green crystals?

Green aventurine glitters with flakes of chromium mica (see the
[green aventurine guide](/blogs/guides/green-aventurine-meaning)). Malachite is banded in light and dark green,
coloured by copper. It should stay dry and never be cut or sanded at home. Jade covers two minerals, nephrite and
jadeite, and much "jade" on sale is dyed quartzite or serpentine. Amazonite is a blue-green feldspar, and moss
agate is clear with green threads like weed in a pond. In folklore, green stones stand for growth, luck and
fresh starts.

## What are the yellow and orange crystals?

Citrine runs from lemon to burnt orange, and most of it is heated amethyst (see the
[citrine guide](/blogs/guides/citrine-meaning)). Carnelian is a translucent orange chalcedony
([carnelian guide](/blogs/guides/carnelian-meaning)). Tiger's eye is golden brown with a moving band of light
([tiger's eye guide](/blogs/guides/tigers-eye-meaning)). Yellow and orange calcite are soft, waxy and inexpensive.
These warm colours are traditionally linked with confidence and creativity.

## What are the red crystals?

Garnet is the deep wine red of January's birthstone. Red jasper is opaque brick red, coloured by iron oxide.
Ruby is red corundum and sits with gemstones rather than crystals. Red stones are traditionally associated with
courage and passion, and they suit a Valentine's gift that isn't pink.

## What are the black and brown crystals?

Black tourmaline is ridged and glossy (see the [black tourmaline guide](/blogs/guides/black-tourmaline-meaning)).
Obsidian is volcanic glass. Smoky quartz runs from pale brown to near black, and some is clear quartz irradiated
to darken it. Black onyx is usually dyed chalcedony. Polished hematite is metallic grey, and most "magnetic
hematite" beads are a man-made magnetic material rather than the mineral. In crystal lore, dark stones are seen
as grounding and protective.

## What are the white and clear crystals?

Clear quartz is colourless and glassy ([clear quartz guide](/blogs/guides/clear-quartz-meaning)). Selenite is
silky white gypsum that must stay dry ([selenite guide](/blogs/guides/selenite-meaning)). Howlite is white with
grey veins and is often dyed to imitate turquoise. Moonstone is milky with a floating glow
([moonstone guide](/blogs/guides/moonstone-meaning)). White stones are traditionally linked with clarity and new
beginnings.

## Which crystals change colour as you move them?

Labradorite flashes blue, green and gold from grey (see the [labradorite guide](/blogs/guides/labradorite-meaning)).
Opal shows a play of colour from inside. Moonstone glows blue-white across its surface. These suit someone who
likes a stone that looks different every time they pick it up.

## How do you choose a crystal by colour?

Look at what they wear and how their home is decorated. Someone in navy and gold will like lapis. Someone with
a white flat might prefer selenite or clear quartz to anything bright. If they wear one colour all the time, a
stone in that colour is a safe and personal choice. The [crystals collection](/collections/crystals) filters by
colour.
